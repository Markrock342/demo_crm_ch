# Demo photo credits

Each one was resized, and some were cropped, then converted to WebP. The exact change is listed for each photo.
CC BY and CC BY-SA licenses require attribution. CC BY-SA also requires that the modified images (the files in this folder) stay under the same license.
CC0 photos need no attribution. They are credited here anyway.
