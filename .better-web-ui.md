## Design Context

### Users
Chinese logistics operators on the China–Thailand corridor (freight forwarding, import/export, warehouse). Used in bright meeting rooms during a sales pitch.

### Brand Personality
ledger-house, measured, ink-and-seal

### Aesthetic Direction
Warm paper field, ink type, one cinnabar chop-stamp accent. Light theme only.

### Design Principles
Chinese-first copy. Tabular cargo numerals. Elevation only when it ranks. AI lives in the inbox, never as a sparkle badge.
